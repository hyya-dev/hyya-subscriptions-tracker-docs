# Changelog

Each store release, with what was new in it. Releases whose only note was "general bug fixes and stability improvements" are listed by version alone. Dates are when the release went live; from 2.7.1 on, each store is dated separately.

## 2.7.1 (App Store 2026-11-04, Google Play 2026-11-04)
General bug fixes and stability improvements.

## 2.7.0 (2026-11-01)
New: home screen widgets and an easy way to turn on renewal reminders.

## 2.6.1 (2026-10-29)
General bug fixes and stability improvements.

## 2.6.0 (2026-10-27)
Now made for iPhone Duo.

## 2.5.0 (2026-10-23)
Add your first subscription in one tap.

## 2.4.0 (2026-10-21), 2.3.0 (2026-10-17), 2.2.2 (2026-10-13), 2.2.1 and 2.2.0 (October 2026)
General bug fixes and stability improvements.

## 2.1.7 (2026-09-24)
Subscriptions you cancel are now kept under Subscriptions › Cancelled, together with how much you save by no longer paying for each one. Changed your mind? One tap moves that subscription back to your active list.

## 2.1.6 (2026-09-16)
General bug fixes and stability improvements.

## 2.1.5 (2026-09-09)
- The Pro and Pro+ plans are easier to find: they now sit at the top of Settings.
- The mascot on those cards now matches the other hYYa cards, and the add button no longer covers a subscription's delete icon.

## 2.1.3 (2026-09-08)
- Plans are clearer: Pro is a one-time unlock, and Pro+ is the monthly or yearly subscription that adds more forwarded receipts every month. Both sit under Settings › Plans.
- If Pro+ isn't offered in your country yet, the app now says so instead of reporting a store error.
- Your plan status now refreshes reliably every time the app opens.

## 2.1.2 (2026-09-02)
- Reminders now follow your device's time zone, so "three days before" means exactly that.
- Fixed: reaching the 5-subscription limit showed an error instead of the option to upgrade.

## 2.1.1 (August 2026)
- Choosing a plan is simpler: the Pro+ screen is shorter, with monthly and yearly in view together.
- Fixed: a promotional card could cover part of the subscription details.

## 2.1.0 (August 2026)
- Receipts included with your plan no longer expire. They stay in your balance until you use them.
- Forward a renewal and the app updates the price it tracks, so an increase never slips past you.

## 2.0.0 (2026-08-16)
- Receipts forwarded as an attachment now work.
- Much more accurate tracking: every amount and date is checked against the receipt itself.
- Pro+ introduced, as an optional subscription for more receipt reading.
- Fixed: taking a photo of a receipt, and choosing one from your library.

## 1.0.3
First release.
