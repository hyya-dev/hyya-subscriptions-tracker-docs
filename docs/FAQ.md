## Getting started

**How do I sign in?**
Enter your email address. We send a 6-digit code to it, and the app signs you in when you type it. There is no password.

**How do I add a subscription?**
Three ways:
- **Forward the receipt email** to `receipts@hst.hyya.com` from the address you signed up with.
- **Photograph a paper receipt** with the app's camera button.
- **Add it by hand.**

Forwarded and photographed receipts are read automatically, and the details are checked against the receipt before the subscription is added.

**Can I forward receipts from another email address?**
Yes. Add up to 5 more addresses in **Settings › Forwarding addresses**. We email each one a confirmation link, and the address works once you open it. The link expires after 24 hours.

## Reminders

**How do I get reminders?**
Turn on **Payment Reminders** in **Settings › Notifications**, and allow notifications when your phone asks. Then choose how early to be reminded, from 1 day to 2 weeks.

**Why didn't I get a reminder for a bill?**
- **Reminders or notifications are off.** Check that Payment Reminders is on in Settings and that your phone allows notifications from the app.
- **The bill was already due sooner than your setting** when you added it. Its first reminder comes in the next cycle.
- **It's a weekly bill.** Weekly bills are reminded at most 2 days before, whatever the setting.

## Forwarded receipts

**I forwarded a receipt, but no subscription appeared. Why?**
- **The sender address isn't on your account.** Forward from the address you signed up with, or add it under Forwarding addresses.
- **It was a one-time purchase.** One-off payments are saved as receipts but don't become subscriptions, and the app tells you so.
- **It's already tracked.** A receipt for a subscription you already have updates that one instead of adding a second.
- **You're at the free plan's limit of 5 subscriptions.** The receipt is kept, but nothing new is added until you upgrade or remove a subscription.

**What happens if I forward a cancellation email?**
The app marks the matching subscription as cancelled and moves it to **Subscriptions › Cancelled**. If you have more than one plan from the same company, it doesn't guess: it tells you, so you can cancel the right one in the app.

## Plans

**What's the difference between Pro and Pro+?**
- **Pro** is a one-time purchase: unlimited subscriptions, for good.
- **Pro+** is a monthly or yearly subscription: everything in Pro, plus more forwarded receipts every month. Unused ones carry over and never expire.

The free plan has every feature and tracks up to 5 subscriptions.

**What happens if I cancel Pro+?**
You keep the forwarded receipts already in your balance. The monthly top-up stops, and your account goes back to the free plan's limit of 5 subscriptions, unless you also own Pro.

**I bought a plan but the app doesn't show it.**
Use **Restore purchases** under **Settings › Plans**. Purchases belong to the store you bought them from: an App Store purchase unlocks the app on iPhone, and a Google Play purchase unlocks it on Android.

## Privacy and your data

**Does the app connect to my bank?**
No. It never asks for bank or card details. It only sees the receipts you choose to forward or photograph.

**Can I lock the app?**
Yes. Turn on **Biometric Lock** in **Settings › Security**. It also hides amounts on the home-screen widget.

**Can I export or delete my data?**
Export everything as a spreadsheet with **Settings › Export to CSV**. Delete your account and all its data with **Settings › Delete Account**.

**Does my data follow me to a new phone?**
Yes. Sign in with the same email address on any device to see your subscriptions.
