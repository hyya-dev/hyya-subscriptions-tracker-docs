## What it does

| | |
|---|---|
| 📨 **Forward a receipt** | Forward a receipt email, or photograph a paper one, and the app fills in the details and tracks the renewal |
| ✍️ **Or add it by hand** | Weekly, monthly or yearly, in 26 currencies, with totals in the one you choose |
| 🔔 **Reminders you set** | A notification 1 day to 2 weeks before each renewal, once you turn reminders on |
| 📅 **See what's due** | Monthly and yearly totals, a calendar of upcoming bills, and home-screen widgets |
| 🗂️ **Cancelled, not forgotten** | Cancelled subscriptions move to their own list, with what you save by no longer paying |
| 🔒 **No bank access** | It never asks for bank or card details. It only sees the receipts you choose to send it |

## Platforms

- **iPhone**: iOS 15 or later (home-screen widgets need iOS 17). App Store.
- **Android**: Android 7.0 or later. Google Play.

## Pricing

Free to download, with every feature included. The free plan tracks up to 5 subscriptions and includes a few forwarded receipts to try.

- **hYYa Pro**: a one-time purchase. Unlimited subscriptions, for good.
- **hYYa Pro+**: a monthly or yearly subscription. Everything in Pro, plus more forwarded receipts every month. Unused ones carry over and never expire.

Purchases belong to the store you bought them from: an App Store purchase unlocks the app on iPhone, and a Google Play purchase unlocks it on Android.

## Privacy

There is no bank or card connection. The app sees only the receipts you forward or photograph, and you can delete your account and its data from Settings at any time. Full policy: [hyya.com/privacypolicy/hyya-subscriptions-tracker.html](https://hyya.com/privacypolicy/hyya-subscriptions-tracker.html).
